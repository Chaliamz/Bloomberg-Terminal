Board state §53 · §54 information standard

Every value slot on this board reads UNFED because no primary source is connected. That is the terminal working correctly, not failing. What the board shows instead is the wiring: which engines hold live logic, which are still specification, and exactly which institution's feed fills each gap. Engine rack — · logic vs feed

Geopolitical transmission chain §12 · never report “tensions increased”

The chain is the output, not the headline. A missile test is not a market event until it is priced through physical supply. If a link cannot be established — no affected supply, no commodity channel — the event scores high on attention and low on market relevance, and the board says so rather than manufacturing a narrative.

Priority score model §45

Credibility gate
priority = core × (0.25 + 0.75 × cred/100)

Multiplicative, not additive. An unverifiable claim keeps at most a quarter of its raw score — so “sources say emergency 100bp cut” from an anonymous account cannot outrank a confirmed print.

Novelty (§45) is handled by the duplicate filter; market sensitivity is folded into impact. Neither is yet a separate input.

Regime matrix §39 · §41 · all six undetermined

Regime decides the sign, not the data. The same weak payroll is bought under a monetary-driven regime and sold under a growth-driven one. Until these six resolve, every directional cell in the cross-asset map returns AMBIGUOUS at zero confidence.

Master question §55 · asked of every event

  1. What did the market believe before this arrived?
  2. What new information actually arrived?
  3. How much does it move the distribution of outcomes?
  4. What should react, on theory?
  5. What did react?
  6. Where is positioning vulnerable?
  7. Where is liquidity concentrated?
  8. What is already priced?
  9. What remains unpriced?
  10. What is the next confirmation signal?
  11. What would invalidate the thesis?

Question 5 minus question 4 is the highest-value output the terminal produces — and the one engine still unbuilt (§34).

Information standard §53 · these six never mix

Fact

Directly verified against the issuing institution's own document.

Data

Quantitative observation with a unit, a timestamp and a source.

Analysis

Interpretation of fact and data. Carries a confidence.

Inference

System-generated conclusion. Never given the weight of a fact.

Scenario

Conditional outcome with an explicit trigger and invalidation.

Rumor

Unverified. Labelled, quarantined, excluded from any decision.
