Live catalyst — cross-asset transmission August payrolls +162k vs +53k expected

  1. Growth impulse is stronger than expected.
  2. In an inflation-dominant regime the policy path is the binding constraint, so stronger growth means later/less easing: yields rise.
  3. Good news is therefore sold in equities and bad news is bought - the stock/bond correlation is positive.
  4. This holds only while growth is far enough above stall speed. Once weak data starts threatening earnings, the regime itself flips and the sign of the equity response inverts.
